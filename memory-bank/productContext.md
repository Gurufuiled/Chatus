# Product Context

## Current Product Direction
- The app is still aimed at a strong Telegram-like messenger experience first.
- Group chats are no longer just planned; they are now part of the real product surface.
- Private channels are still planned, but the current business direction is:
  - internal billing handles payment
  - billing updates the user's `Loginus account type`
  - account type determines which creation/access capabilities unlock

## What Users Should Feel
- Private chats and group chats should feel familiar and low-friction.
- Common Telegram-like interactions matter:
  - reply
  - forward
  - selection mode
  - profile navigation
  - group management
  - media/files/voice access
- Group chat should not feel like a weak "phase 2" surface; it should feel like a normal first-class messenger surface.

## Current Group Scope
- create a group chat from the chats area
- open group profile from the chat header
- edit group photo/title/description if you are the owner
- add members if you are the owner
- leave group / delete group
- long-press participants for write/tag/remove actions
- browse group participants / media / files / voice

## Near-Term Product Priorities
1. Keep improving group chats until they feel familiar and dependable.
2. Continue polishing calls and the rest of the messenger surface.
3. After that, move into private channels + account-type capability gating.
4. Later sprint items like circles, video calls, mini app, and bots can build on a stronger messenger base.

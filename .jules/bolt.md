## 2024-06-17 - React.memo with inline callbacks in FlatList
**Learning:** `ContactResultRow` was not wrapped in `React.memo()`, and its `onPress` prop was receiving an inline arrow function from `ChatListScreen`'s `renderItem`. This caused all contact rows to needlessly re-render whenever the list rendered, nullifying potential list optimizations.
**Action:** When mapping over items in a `FlatList` where child components expect callbacks with arguments, ensure the child is wrapped in `React.memo()`, and pass stable callback references (like directly passing a `useCallback` wrapper) from the parent instead of wrapping them in inline arrow functions that recreate references on every render.

## 2026-07-06 - Memoizing FlatList Props
**Learning:** Passing inline arrays to FlatList's `data` prop or inline functions to `renderItem` causes the entire list to re-render unnecessarily on every parent component update, leading to performance bottlenecks.
**Action:** Always extract and memoize FlatList props like `data`, `renderItem`, and `ListHeaderComponent` using `useMemo` and `useCallback`.
